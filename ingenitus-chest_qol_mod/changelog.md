# Changelog

## [1.0.0] – 2026-09-25

- Initial release. Adds a **Chests** tab to the settings menu; every option can be switched off.
- **Open on touch** – touching a chest opens it without pressing interact.
- **Loot on touch** – with the vanilla One-Click Looting upgrade owned, touching a chest loots it
  straight to your inventory.
- **Protective-ring reach** – every generated chest sits inside eight protective tiles. Once at
  least one of them is mined, standing on any of those tiles takes the chest; an intact ring never
  gives it away through solid rock.
- **No outclassed scrolls** – a chest power-up scroll for a weapon no stronger than one of the same
  kind you already own becomes a passive-upgrade card (same multiplier vanilla uses). Tiers are read
  from the weapon's file name, so every weapon line is covered (including electric pickaxes).
  Works alongside `der_floh-passive_drop_mod`.
